**Goal:** build 25-year and 100-year **design storms** from IDF depths with HEC-HMS's Frequency Storm method, and compare their floods with the observed storm.

**You need:** your project with `Teaching_Catchment` (L02). **Goes with:** Session 8. **Time:** about 40 minutes.

Depths from the IDF equation $i = K T^x/(D+a)^n$ with the illustrative constants $K = 6.93$, $x = 0.189$, $a = 0.5$, $n = 0.878$ (depth = $i \times D$):

| Duration | 5 min | 15 min | 1 h | 2 h | 3 h | 6 h |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T = 25 yr (mm) | 17.0 | 41.0 | 89.2 | 113.9 | 127.2 | 147.7 |
| T = 100 yr (mm) | 22.1 | 53.3 | 115.9 | 148.0 | 165.3 | 191.9 |

<!--step: Check two depths yourself-->
Use the IDF tool in Session 8 (or a calculator) to confirm the 25-year 1-hour and 6-hour depths. Never type numbers into a model that you have not checked.

<!--step: Create the design-storm met model-->
1. <span class="menu">Components › Create Component › Meteorologic Model</span>; name `Design_T25`.
2. **Precipitation = Frequency Storm**. **Basins** tab: Include Subbasins = **Yes**.

<!--step: Set the storm parameters-->
Click the met model's **Frequency Storm** node and set:

| Parameter | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Probability / exceedance | **4 %** | 1/T for T = 25 years |
| Input type | Annual Duration | our depths come from annual maxima |
| Output type | Annual Duration | |
| Intensity duration | **15 minutes** | the shortest burst built into the storm |
| Storm duration | **6 hours** | |
| Intensity position | **50 %** | peak burst in the middle of the storm |
| Storm area / area reduction | 54.85 km², or none | no areal reduction for this small catchment |

> [!note] Field names
> The exact labels and the list of options differ a little between HEC-HMS 4.x versions. Match the meaning, not the exact words.

<!--step: Enter the depths-->
Enter the 25-year depths (17.0, 41.0, 89.2, 113.9, 127.2, 147.7 mm) against 5 min, 15 min, 1 h, 2 h, 3 h and 6 h. HEC-HMS asks only for durations up to the storm duration.

<!--step: Run it-->
1. Make control specs `Design24h`: `01Jan2000 00:00` to `02Jan2000 00:00`, interval **5 minutes**.
2. Create and compute `Run_T25` (Teaching_Catchment, Design_T25, Design24h).

> [!see] You should see
> A symmetrical "balanced" hyetograph: the most intense 15 minutes in the middle, the most intense hour around it, and so on — every duration matches its IDF depth.

<!--step: Build the 100-year storm and compare-->
1. Copy `Design_T25` to `Design_T100`: probability **1 %**, depths 22.1, 53.3, 115.9, 148.0, 165.3, 191.9 mm.
2. Run `Run_T100` and fill in:

| Run | 6-h rain (mm) | Peak outflow (m³/s) | Time of peak |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Observed (`Run_S03`) | 105 | | |
| `Run_T25` | 147.7 | | |
| `Run_T100` | 191.9 | | |

> [!hms] What to notice
> The 25-year design storm has only about 40 % more rain than the observed storm, but its 15-minute burst is far more intense, so the peak rises by much more than 40 %. Design is driven by the burst, not the total.

<!--end-->
