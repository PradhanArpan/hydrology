## Theory topics

- **Extreme hydrologic events; flood risk definition in hydraulic design (spillways, bridges, culverts)**: Extreme hydrologic events; flood risk definition in hydraulic design (spillways, bridges, culverts)
- **Annual Maximum Flood (AMF) series vs Partial Duration / Peak-Over-Threshold (POT) series**: Annual Maximum Flood (AMF) series vs Partial Duration / Peak-Over-Threshold (POT) series
- **Return period T definition**: recurrence interval T = 1 / P(X >= x); exceedance probability P and non-exceedance probability q
- **Plotting position formulas**: Weibull: P = m / (N + 1); Hazen: P = (2m - 1) / (2N); California: P = m / N; Cunnane: P = (m - 0.4)/(N + 0.2)
- **Probability plotting papers**: normal, log-normal, extreme value grids

## Worked numerical problem types

1. Ranking an annual maximum flood series and calculating Weibull, Hazen, and Cunnane plotting positions
1. Calculating return period and annual probability of exceedance for observed historical peaks
1. Hydrologic risk analysis: probability of at least one exceedance of design flood during structure design life of n years: R = 1 - (1 - 1/T)^n
