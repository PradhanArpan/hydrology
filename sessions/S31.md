## Theory topics

- **Concept of stage-discharge relation (rating curve); control types**: section control and channel control
- **Mathematical power form of rating curve**: Q = Cr * (G - a)^b, where G is gauge reading and a is stage at zero flow (PZF)
- **Techniques for determining zero-flow stage 'a'**: trial and error, arithmetic plotting, running 3-point regression
- **Logarithmic transformation and least-squares linear regression**: ln Q = ln Cr + b * ln(G - a)
- **Confidence limits and correlation coefficient requirements for valid hydrometric rating curves**: Confidence limits and correlation coefficient requirements for valid hydrometric rating curves

## Worked numerical problem types

1. Determination of stage at zero flow 'a' using the arithmetic graphical method
1. Least-squares logarithmic regression to estimate rating curve parameters Cr and exponent b
1. Discharge prediction for design flood stage with 95% confidence interval estimation
