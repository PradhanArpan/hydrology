## Theory topics

- **Concept of area-velocity method; velocity distribution in an open channel vertical**: Concept of area-velocity method; velocity distribution in an open channel vertical
- **Point velocity measurement**: 0.6d single-point and 0.2d/0.8d two-point methods
- **Current meters**: Price type AA cup meter, Pygmy meter, horizontal axis propeller meter (Ott)
- **Current meter rating equation**: v = a + b*N; calibration in towing tanks
- **Discharge computation methods**: Mid-section method and Mean-section method (IS:1192 / ISO 748)

## Worked numerical problem types

1. Calculation of channel discharge from field current meter sounding data using the mid-section method
1. Mean-section method calculation comparing discharge with mid-section results
1. Current meter calibration equation fitting: solving calibration constants a and b from rating tank data
