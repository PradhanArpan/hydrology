> [!note] Key ideas
> - For ungauged catchments without streamflow records, **Synthetic Unit Hydrographs (SUH)** relate hydrograph shape parameters to measurable physical basin characteristics.
> - **Snyder's Synthetic Unit Hydrograph (1938)** uses basin length $L$, centroidal length $L_{ca}$, and regional empirical coefficients $C_t$ ($1.35	ext{--}1.65$) and $C_p$ ($0.56	ext{--}0.69$).
> - Key formulas:
>   - Basin lag: $t_p = C_t (L \cdot L_{ca})^{0.3}$
>   - Standard storm duration: $t_r = t_p / 5.5$
>   - Adjusted lag for non-standard duration $t_R$: $t_{pR} = t_p + 0.25 (t_R - t_r)$
>   - Peak discharge: $Q_{pR} = rac{2.78 C_p A}{t_{pR}}$ ($	ext{m}^3	ext{/s}$)
>   - Hydrograph widths at $50\%$ and $75\%$ peak: $W_{50} = rac{5.87}{(q_{pR})^{1.08}}$, $W_{75} = rac{W_{50}}{1.75}$.

## 1. Snyder's Synthetic Unit Hydrograph Equations

Snyder analyzed Appalachian basins in the USA, establishing relationships between catchment geometry and the three key points of a unit hydrograph (lag time, peak flow, and time base).

1. **Basin Lag Time ($t_p$):**
$$t_p = C_t (L \cdot L_{ca})^{0.3}	ext{ (hours)}$$
Where:
- $L$ = stream length along main channel from basin divide to outlet ($	ext{km}$)
- $L_{ca}$ = distance along main stream from outlet to point opposite basin centroid ($	ext{km}$)
- $C_t$ = regional physiographic coefficient ($1.35$ to $1.65$ in Indian sub-basins)

2. **Standard Duration ($t_r$):**
$$t_r = rac{t_p}{5.5}	ext{ (hours)}$$

3. **Modified Lag for Design Duration $t_R$ ($t_R 
e t_r$):**
$$t_{pR} = t_p + rac{1}{4} (t_R - t_r)$$

4. **Peak Discharge ($Q_{pR}$):**
$$Q_{pR} = rac{2.78 \cdot C_p \cdot A}{t_{pR}}	ext{ (m}^3	ext{/s)}$$
Peak discharge per unit area:
$$q_{pR} = rac{Q_{pR}}{A} = rac{2.78 \cdot C_p}{t_{pR}}	ext{ (m}^3	ext{/s/km}^2	ext{)}$$

5. **Time Base ($t_b$):**
$$t_b = 3 + 3 \left(rac{t_{pR}}{24}ight)	ext{ (days)} = 72 + 3 t_{pR}	ext{ (hours for large basins)}$$
For small basins, $t_b$ is frequently taken as $3$ to $5$ times $t_{pR}$.

6. **US Army Corps Width Equations:**
$$W_{50} = rac{5.87}{(q_{pR})^{1.08}}	ext{ (hours)}$$
$$W_{75} = rac{W_{50}}{1.75}	ext{ (hours)}$$

## 2. Worked Numerical Examples

### Worked Example 24.1: Snyder's SUH Derivation

**Problem:** A $520	ext{ km}^2$ ungauged catchment has main stream length $L = 35	ext{ km}$ and centroidal distance $L_{ca} = 18	ext{ km}$. Regional parameters are $C_t = 1.5$ and $C_p = 0.65$. Derive the parameters of a 4-hour synthetic unit hydrograph.

**Solution:**

**Step 1: Basin lag time $t_p$**
$$L \cdot L_{ca} = 35 	imes 18 = 630	ext{ km}^2$$
$$(630)^{0.3} = 6.901$$
$$t_p = 1.5 	imes 6.901 = 10.35	ext{ hours}$$

**Step 2: Standard duration $t_r$**
$$t_r = rac{10.35}{5.5} = 1.88	ext{ hours}$$

**Step 3: Adjusted lag for $t_R = 4.0	ext{ hours}$**
$$t_{pR} = 10.35 + 0.25 (4.0 - 1.88) = 10.35 + 0.25(2.12) = 10.35 + 0.53 = 10.88	ext{ hours}$$

**Step 4: Peak discharge $Q_{pR}$**
$$Q_{pR} = rac{2.78 \cdot 0.65 \cdot 520}{10.88} = rac{939.64}{10.88} = 86.36	ext{ m}^3	ext{/s}$$
$$q_{pR} = rac{86.36}{520} = 0.1661	ext{ m}^3	ext{/s/km}^2$$

### Session 24 recap
1. Snyder's method synthesizes a UH from $L$ and $L_{ca}$.
2. Standard duration: $t_r = t_p / 5.5$.
3. Adjusted lag: $t_{pR} = t_p + 0.25(t_R - t_r)$.
4. Peak flow: $Q_{pR} = 2.78 C_p A / t_{pR}$.
