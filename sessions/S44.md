## Theory topics

- **Total drawdown in a pumping well**: s_w = s_aquifer + s_well = B * Q + C * Q^2 (Jacob's equation)
- **Aquifer loss coefficient B (laminar flow) vs well loss coefficient C (turbulent flow through screen and casing)**: Aquifer loss coefficient B (laminar flow) vs well loss coefficient C (turbulent flow through screen and casing)
- **Step-drawdown pumping test for evaluating well performance; well efficiency E_w = (B * Q / s_w) * 100%**: Step-drawdown pumping test for evaluating well performance; well efficiency E_w = (B * Q / s_w) * 100%
- **Specific capacity of a well**: SC = Q / s_w; relation between specific capacity and transmissivity
- **Open wells in unconfined formations; recuperation test principle**: specific yield of open well K/A = (2.303 / t) * log10(s1 / s2)

## Worked numerical problem types

1. Determination of formation loss B and well loss C from multi-step drawdown test data
1. Well efficiency calculation at multiple operating pumping discharges
1. Recuperation test calculation: computing specific capacity factor (K/A) and safe yield of an open dug well
