## Theory topics

- **Three-parameter distributions in flood hydrology; logarithmic transformation z = log10(x)**: Three-parameter distributions in flood hydrology; logarithmic transformation z = log10(x)
- **Sample statistics in log-space**: mean z_mean, standard deviation s_z, and skewness coefficient C_s
- **Log-Pearson Type III frequency equation**: log10(x_T) = z_mean + K_z * s_z, where K_z = f(C_s, T)
- **Guidelines for flood frequency analysis**: US Geological Survey Bulletin 17C / CWC guidelines
- **Comparing Gumbel vs LP3; handling outliers (high and low outliers), regional skew coefficients**: Comparing Gumbel vs LP3; handling outliers (high and low outliers), regional skew coefficients
