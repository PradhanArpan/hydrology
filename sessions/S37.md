## Theory topics

- **Role of groundwater in the hydrologic cycle; vertical distribution of subsurface water**: Role of groundwater in the hydrologic cycle; vertical distribution of subsurface water
- **Zone of aeration (unsaturated zone)**: soil water zone, intermediate vadose zone, capillary fringe
- **Zone of saturation (phreatic zone); water table definition and phreatic surface**: Zone of saturation (phreatic zone); water table definition and phreatic surface
- **Aquifer classifications**: unconfined (water-table), confined (artesian), semi-confined (leaky), perched aquifers; aquitard, aquiclude, aquifuge
- **Hydrogeological properties**: porosity n, specific yield S_y, specific retention S_r, and their fundamental relation: n = S_y + S_r

## Worked numerical problem types

1. Calculation of porosity, void ratio, and water volume stored in a saturated aquifer column
1. Determination of specific yield and volume of drainable water released from water table decline
1. Calculation of specific retention from gravimetric soil moisture measurement
