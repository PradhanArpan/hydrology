## About the portal's illustrations and data

- All illustrations and interactive tools were drawn for this portal; they are schematic, not to scale unless stated.
- Datasets named `U1_*` are **synthetic teaching data** (generator scripts in the repository `tools/` folder). Numerical examples marked *Slide* reproduce the problems of the CE533 lecture slides.
- Software screens shown are schematics, not screenshots; menu names follow HEC-HMS 4.x and QGIS LTR and may differ slightly between versions.
