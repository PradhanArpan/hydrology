## What the examinations test

The mid-semester and end-semester examinations are **written, closed-book** papers. They test the material in the **Theory** tab of each session: definitions, explanations with sketches, derivations and numerical problems. Software is not examined directly, but the labs are designed so that you understand the numbers in those problems.

Each session's **Practice** tab contains 2-, 5- and 10-mark questions in the examination style. Solve them on paper, in the time you would have in the exam.

## Session work (CIA I)

- Each lab ends with a short **Submit** item. Post it to the matching Google Classroom assignment before the next class.
- The quizzes in the portal are for self-checking and are not graded; graded quizzes are set in Google Classroom.
- Participation marks come from in-class activities: being present is not enough — the activity sheet or file must be submitted.

## Classroom policies

- Laptops are allowed only for subject-related activities. Bring one with HEC-HMS installed to every class from Session 2.
- **AI-use log:** every AI-assisted submission must include the prompt used, the output obtained, your verification, the corrected calculation and a short reflection. Unverified AI output will not be accepted as engineering reasoning.
- **Data citation:** hydrological data in assignments and the project must be cited with source (IMD, India-WRIS, KSNDMC, CGWB or field measurement).
- Plagiarism-free submissions; integrity of hydrological data is treated as professional ethics.
